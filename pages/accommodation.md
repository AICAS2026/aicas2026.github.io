- Address: Area 2, Ha Long Street, Bai Chay Ward, Ha Long City, Quang Ninh Province 
- Number of rooms: 200
- Distance from the conference venue: 1.3km (18-min walk/2-min by taxi)

Overlooking Hạ Long Bay, this polished hotel is a short walk from
shops and restaurants, 1 km from the nearest beach, and 7 km from Long
Tien Temple. Modern, warm rooms come with free Wi-Fi, TVs, and

#### Recommendations

- **Shortest commute:** Mövenpick Living West Hanoi, followed by HANA STAY and Novotel Suites Hanoi.
- **Best nearby full-service hotels:** Mövenpick Living West Hanoi and Novotel Suites Hanoi combine short transfers with pools, fitness, dining, and business facilities.
- **Best luxury balance:** Lotte Hotel Hanoi combines five-star facilities and panoramic views with a relatively manageable drive.
- **Best for a longer business stay:** The Bloom Hanoi and HANA STAY offer apartment-style layouts with kitchens and remain close to the destination.
- **Best central luxury stays:** Le Chanvre suits the Old Quarter, while Apricot Hotel offers an art-led experience beside Hoan Kiem Lake.
- **Best Old Quarter value among quoted rates:** Holiday Suites has the lowest quoted rate and includes breakfast, although VAT is extra and the property name should be confirmed.
