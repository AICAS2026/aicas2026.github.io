## Keynote #1: From Design Automation to Autonomous Design: AI Super Agents for Chip Development
**Ziyad Hanna**<br/>
*Corporate VP, System Design Verification and AI Technologies at Cadence Design Systems*

**Abstract:** AI is already improving productivity in many tasks in
chip design and verification, but today’s solutions remain largely
assistant-driven and task-specific. The next step is to evolve toward
agentic AI systems that can plan, execute, evaluate, and refine
complex design workflows across multiple tools and engineering
domains. This talk examines how Super Agents, coordinated teams of
specialized AI agents, can accelerate chip design and validation
through iterative, closed-loop execution tightly integrated with EDA
tools, engines, and design data. It will discuss potential
architectures, including agent orchestration, multi-agent
collaboration, EDA harnesses, and human-in-the-loop control, as well
as the practical challenges of reliability, verification, security,
data governance, and deployment at scale.

**Biography:**
Ziyad Hanna is a distinguished global technology executive, computer
scientist, and AI leader, with more than 35 years of experience
shaping the fields of electronic design automation and artificial
intelligence. As a Corporate Vice President of Cadence Design Systems,
and R&D General Manager, leading the company's technology innovations
and global business with focus on software and AI technologies for the
worldwide chip design industry. In 2025, Ziyad Hanna was appointed to
lead Cadence's global artificial intelligence strategy, a role that
underscores the company's deep trust in his visionary leadership and
technical excellence. As the strategic head of AI at Cadence, Ziyad is
responsible for shaping and executing the company's direction in
artificial intelligence—driving innovation across global teams,
identifying transformative opportunities, and steering technological
advancement in one of the most critical areas of the future chip
design ecosystem. His leadership in this role is pivotal to Cadence's
evolution as a front-runner in AI-driven design and verification
technologies. Prior to Cadence, he was the Sr. Vice President, Chief
Scientist and General Manager of Jasper the first startup company that
made its way to exit and managed by an Arab Israeli General
Manager. While being a US permanent resident and leading the company
from the Silicon Valley, Jasper earned the Red Herring Top 100 North
America award in 2013 and was acquired by Cadence in June 2014. Prior
to joining Jasper, Ziyad was Senior Principal Engineer and Group
Leader in the Design and Technology division at Intel Haifa. He played
a key role in developing computer aided design (CAD) systems that were
deployed across nearly all Intel microprocessor designs since the
early 1990s. A senior IEEE member and a lecturer in the EECS
department at the Technion Israel, Ziyad is very active in the Hi-Tech
field and has participated in many international conferences and
workshops. He has published more than 30 articles, earned 25 patents,
and gave hundreds of talks, a frequent keynote speaker, and respected
advisor in various fields in computer science and chaired conferences
including the annual Jasper User Group (JUG) for 15 consecutive
years. Ziyad received numerous awards including Intel's highest
achievement award twice, and Cadence move-the-needle award. He
obtained his bachelor's and master's degree from Tel Aviv University,
and PhD in computer science from Oxford University in England, where
he serves as a visiting professor in the computer science
department. Ziyad is very active to foster the Hi-Tech in the Arab
community. Ziyad was ranked number 39 in The-Marker's 100 most
influential people on the Israel life for year 2019, top the list in
the Hi-Tech category. Also, received honorable doctorate from Rubin
collage, and his name was included in the list of the most influential
people in Hi-Tech by both The-Marker and People and Computers
magazines.
